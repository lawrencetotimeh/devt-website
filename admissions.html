How registration works

  • Fill out the application form

    Tell us your name, contact info, and which program(s) you're interested in. It takes a few minutes and works fine on a phone.

  • Get your reference code

    As soon as you submit, you'll get a short reference code on the screen — write it down or take a screenshot. If you gave an email, we'll also send it there.

  • DEVT reviews your application

    Our registrar reviews applications and updates your status as it moves along.

  • Check your status anytime

    Come back to this site and use your reference code to see exactly where your application stands — no password needed.

  • Get enrolled

    Once accepted, we'll help you get set up with your student account and show you how to access your classes.
