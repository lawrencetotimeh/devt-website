Our Programs

14 hands-on trades, each ending in a real diploma or certificate

5 Months·Diploma

1. Computer Science & Office Productivity

Computer fundamentals, Word, Excel (including Advanced Excel), Access, PowerPoint, Publisher, professional typing, and internet navigation.

Requirements: must be able to read and write.

2. Light Duty Driving & Spare Parts

Defensive driving, road and traffic signs, and understanding the different parts and spare parts of a car.

Hair braiding and treatment styles, pedicure, facial, manicure, nails, makeup, and hair installation.

Bridal wear and materials, house decor, and decorations for parties, weddings, graduations, and other events.

Pastry, basic cookery, baking, and basic cake making and decoration.

Specialized cake decoration: bowl icing, wedding cakes, chocolate mayonnaise, red velvet, marble, graduation cakes, and more.

5 Months·Diploma

7. Tailoring & Fashion Design

Sewing, machine assembly and operation, cutting and sewing different fabrics and styles, measuring, and an introduction to fashion design.

5 Months·Diploma

8. General Plumbing

Residential and industrial indoor and outdoor plumbing, and sewer management.

5 Months·Diploma

9. Residential Electricity

Core principles and safe practice of residential electrical systems.

5 Months·Diploma

10. Air Conditioning & Refrigeration

Repairing air conditioners and refrigeration systems, and installing AC in residential and industrial settings.

5 Months·Diploma

11. Satellite & CCTV Installation

Installation of satellite and CCTV systems.

3 Months·Certificate

12. Bead Making

Techniques and craft of bead making.

5 Months·Diploma

13. Soap Science

Liquid soap, laundry soap, tile soap, commode cleaner, air freshener, and other detergent products.

5 Months·Diploma

Organizational and people management, public speaking, ethics, values-based leadership, conflict management, and leading in diversity.

Advanced formulas and functions (IF, VLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, and more), data analysis with pivot tables and pivot charts, data visualization and conditional formatting, Power Query for data cleaning and transformation, dashboard design and reporting, and what-if analysis and forecasting.
