Beyond the classroom

Ducor Service Month

Each year, students take what they're learning directly into the community through Ducor Service Month, a series of service initiatives fully funded by DEVT's Founder. Together, they've provided free hot meals to public school students, apron sets to market women, and detergent donations to the Clara Town Health Center, Marvii Sonii Public School, and area mosques and churches, along with free cosmetology services, reaching more than 1,300 residents of District #14 and other parts of Monrovia.
